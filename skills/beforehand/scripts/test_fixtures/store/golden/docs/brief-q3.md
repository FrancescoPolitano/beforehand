# Halden Logistics — Q3 platform brief

Draft proposal covering the dispatch and invoicing modules for the Q3 steering round.

## Scope
- Dispatch confirmations for depot drivers
- Invoicing reconciliation against the general ledger
- Single sign-on for all users at go-live

## Commercial terms
- Three-year term with an annual break clause
- Phased rollout starting with one depot

## Open items
- Offline fallback behaviour during an outage
- Reporting rollup for depot supervisors
