# Halden Logistics — Q3 platform brief (revised draft)

Revised proposal covering the dispatch and invoicing modules for the Q3 steering round.
Synthetic fixture for evaluation.

## Scope
- Dispatch confirmations for depot drivers
- Invoicing reconciliation against the general ledger
- Single sign-on for all users at go-live

## Commercial terms
- Five-year term, no break clause, priced flat for the whole period
- Savings baseline to be agreed with finance after signature
- Phased rollout starting with one depot

## Cutover and operations
- Production cutover on Friday 25 September, out of hours
- Rollback is a restore from the previous night's backup, about four hours end to end
- Support is handled through the standard ticket queue

## Depot rollout and procurement dates
- Depot drivers confirm each dispatch in a new third mobile app; offline confirmation is
  deferred to phase two
- Vendor shortlist closes 10 July, contract award 5 October, go-live 1 September

## Open items
- Reporting rollup for depot supervisors
- Training plan for dispatchers
